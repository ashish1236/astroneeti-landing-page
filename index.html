FAQ

Before you begin.

What is AstroNeeti?

AstroNeeti is an AI astrology and spiritual companion combining Astro Chat, Kundli, daily guidance, Panchang, Kundli Matching, numerology, Japa, mantras and Bhakti experiences.

What can I ask AstroNeeti?

You can ask about career, business, love, relationships, marriage, family, money and personal life questions. Astrology guidance is intended for reflection and should not replace professional medical, legal or financial advice.

Can I change the screenshots shown on this website?

Yes. All image URLs are centralized in site-config.js. Replace a URL there, or change it to a local path such as assets/images/chat.jpg.

Where can I download AstroNeeti?
